Vrksasana

Tree Pose

BalancingBeginner
⏱Hold 30–60 seconds each side
LegsCoreBalance

Overview

Tree Pose is a classic standing balance that builds focus and stability. By rooting through one leg while the other opens to the side, you develop both physical balance and a calm, concentrated mind.

  • ✓Strengthens ankles, calves, and thighs
  • ✓Opens the hips and inner groin
  • ✓Improves balance and proprioception
  • ✓Builds mental focus and concentration
  • ✓Stretches the thoracic spine

How to do it

  1. 1

    Begin in Mountain Pose. Shift your weight onto your left foot.

  2. 2

    Bend your right knee and place the sole of your right foot on your inner left calf or inner left thigh — never on the knee.

  3. 3

    Press your foot and inner thigh against each other for stability.

  4. 4

    Bring your hands to your heart in prayer position, or extend them overhead like branches.

  5. 5

    Fix your gaze on a steady, unmoving point (drishti) in front of you.

  6. 6

    Hold for 30–60 seconds, then slowly lower your foot and repeat on the other side.

⚠What to avoid

  • Placing your foot directly on your knee — this strains the joint

  • Letting your standing hip jut out to the side

  • Holding your breath — soft, rhythmic breathing improves balance

  • Gripping the floor with your toes; spread them wide instead

Pro tips

💡

If you wobble, that's normal — the tiny corrections your body makes are strengthening your stabilizer muscles.

💡

Stand near a wall at first and lightly touch it for support until your balance develops.

💡

Visualize roots growing from your standing foot deep into the earth.
