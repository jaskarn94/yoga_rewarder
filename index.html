Setu Bandha Sarvangasana

Bridge Pose

SupineBeginner
⏱Hold 30–60 seconds, 1–3 rounds
GlutesHamstringsSpineChest

Overview

Bridge Pose is a gentle backbend that strengthens the glutes and hamstrings while opening the chest and spine. It is a versatile pose that can be restful (with a block) or active and strengthening depending on your intention.

  • ✓Strengthens the glutes, hamstrings, and lower back
  • ✓Opens the chest, shoulders, and hip flexors
  • ✓Stimulates abdominal organs and thyroid
  • ✓Reduces anxiety and mild depression
  • ✓Relieves menstrual discomfort

How to do it

  1. 1

    Lie on your back with knees bent, feet flat on the floor hip-width apart, close to your hips.

  2. 2

    Place your arms along your sides, palms flat on the floor.

  3. 3

    On an exhale, press your feet into the floor and lift your hips toward the ceiling.

  4. 4

    Roll your shoulders underneath you and clasp your hands below your back if comfortable.

  5. 5

    Keep your thighs parallel — don't let your knees splay out.

  6. 6

    Hold for 30–60 seconds, breathing into your chest.

  7. 7

    On an exhale, slowly lower your spine vertebra by vertebra down to the mat.

⚠What to avoid

  • Clenching your glutes aggressively — firm, not gripped

  • Letting your knees splay wide apart — keep them hip-width

  • Compressing your neck — keep the curve natural and don't turn your head

  • Throwing your hips up quickly — lift slowly with control

Pro tips

💡

Press through your heels rather than your toes to better activate your hamstrings and glutes.

💡

For a restorative version, place a yoga block under your sacrum at a comfortable height.

💡

To intensify, lift one leg straight up toward the ceiling (single-leg bridge).
