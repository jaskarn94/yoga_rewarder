Virabhadrasana II

Warrior II

StandingBeginner
⏱Hold 30–60 seconds each side
LegsHipsArmsCore

Overview

Warrior II is a wide-legged, open stance that builds lower-body strength and endurance while opening the hips and chest. It embodies a sense of expansion and readiness, making it one of the most iconic yoga poses.

  • ✓Strengthens and stretches the legs and ankles
  • ✓Opens the hips, groins, and chest
  • ✓Builds stamina and concentration
  • ✓Stimulates the abdominal organs
  • ✓Relieves backaches, especially in the second trimester of pregnancy

How to do it

  1. 1

    Step your feet wide apart (about 4 feet). Turn your right foot out 90° and your left foot in slightly.

  2. 2

    Align your right heel with the arch of your left foot.

  3. 3

    Bend your right knee so it sits directly above your right ankle.

  4. 4

    Extend your arms out to the sides at shoulder height, palms facing down.

  5. 5

    Turn your head to gaze over your right fingertips.

  6. 6

    Keep your torso upright — don't lean over the front leg.

  7. 7

    Hold for 30–60 seconds, then straighten and switch sides.

⚠What to avoid

  • Letting your front knee drift inward — press it toward your little toe

  • Leaning your torso forward over the bent leg

  • Letting your back arm drop — keep both arms energetically active

  • Sinking into your hip — lift your lower belly to protect your lower back

Pro tips

💡

Reach strongly through both arms in opposite directions to keep your torso centered.

💡

The front thigh should aim to be parallel to the floor — work toward this over time.

💡

Soften your face and jaw; the pose is powerful, but your expression can be relaxed.
