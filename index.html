Virabhadrasana I

Warrior I

StandingBeginner
⏱Hold 30–60 seconds each side
LegsHipsShouldersCore

Overview

Warrior I is a powerful standing pose that builds strength, stability, and confidence. It opens the chest and lungs while stretching the hip flexors, making it both energizing and grounding.

  • ✓Strengthens legs, glutes, and core
  • ✓Stretches chest, lungs, and shoulders
  • ✓Opens the hip flexors and psoas
  • ✓Develops focus and inner strength
  • ✓Improves balance and circulation

How to do it

  1. 1

    From Mountain Pose, step your left foot back about 3–4 feet.

  2. 2

    Turn your left foot out to a 45-degree angle. Keep your right foot pointing straight ahead.

  3. 3

    Bend your front (right) knee so it stacks directly over your ankle.

  4. 4

    Square your hips toward the front of the mat as much as possible.

  5. 5

    Inhale and sweep your arms overhead, palms facing each other or touching.

  6. 6

    Gently lift your chest and draw your shoulder blades down your back.

  7. 7

    Hold, breathing fully. Switch sides.

⚠What to avoid

  • Letting the front knee collapse inward — keep it tracking over the second toe

  • Overarching your lower back — engage your core and tuck your tailbone slightly

  • Raising your shoulders up toward your ears — keep them relaxed

  • Twisting your back foot so it points straight back; keep the 45° angle

Pro tips

💡

Imagine your hips as headlights — both pointing directly forward.

💡

Press firmly through the outer edge of your back foot for a stable foundation.

💡

On each inhale, grow taller; on each exhale, sink a little deeper into the lunge.
