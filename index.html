Ustrasana

Camel Pose

BackbendIntermediate
⏱Hold 20–40 seconds, 1–2 rounds
SpineChestHip FlexorsShoulders

Overview

Camel Pose is a powerful kneeling backbend that opens the entire front body — chest, abdomen, hip flexors, and quadriceps — while strengthening the back muscles. It can feel emotionally intense due to the vulnerable opening of the chest.

  • ✓Deeply stretches the entire front body
  • ✓Strengthens the back muscles and spine
  • ✓Opens the chest, improving respiratory capacity
  • ✓Stimulates the nervous system and increases energy
  • ✓Stretches the hip flexors and quadriceps

How to do it

  1. 1

    Kneel on your mat with knees hip-width apart. Tuck your toes or point them back.

  2. 2

    Place your hands on your lower back, fingers pointing down, elbows drawing toward each other.

  3. 3

    Inhale to lengthen your spine. On an exhale, begin to lean back.

  4. 4

    Keep your hips stacked over your knees — do not let them shift forward.

  5. 5

    If available, reach your hands back to your heels one at a time.

  6. 6

    Open your chest toward the ceiling. Let your head follow only if your neck is comfortable.

  7. 7

    Hold for 20–40 seconds, then use your core to come back up slowly.

⚠What to avoid

  • Dumping into your lower back — initiate the bend from your thoracic spine

  • Letting your hips drift behind your knees — keep them stacked

  • Forcing your head back if it causes neck strain — keep your chin tucked

  • Coming out of the pose too quickly — rise slowly to avoid dizziness

Pro tips

💡

If your heels are out of reach, use yoga blocks placed on either side of your feet.

💡

Engage your inner thighs and core throughout to protect your lower back.

💡

After Camel, counter with Child's Pose to neutralize the spine.
