Tadasana

Mountain Pose

StandingBeginner
⏱Hold 30–60 seconds
LegsCorePosture

Overview

Mountain Pose is the foundation of all standing poses. It teaches you to stand with steady, intentional alignment and cultivates body awareness. Deceptively simple, it builds the postural habits that carry into every other posture.

  • ✓Improves posture and body alignment
  • ✓Strengthens thighs, knees, and ankles
  • ✓Firms abdomen and buttocks
  • ✓Relieves sciatica and flat feet
  • ✓Cultivates focus and steady breathing

How to do it

  1. 1

    Stand with your feet together, big toes touching and heels slightly apart.

  2. 2

    Distribute your weight evenly across all four corners of each foot.

  3. 3

    Engage your quadriceps and lift your kneecaps without locking your knees.

  4. 4

    Draw your lower belly gently in and up, lengthening your tailbone toward the floor.

  5. 5

    Roll your shoulders back and down, broadening across your chest.

  6. 6

    Let your arms hang naturally at your sides, palms facing forward.

  7. 7

    Lengthen the back of your neck and gaze softly forward. Breathe steadily for 30–60 seconds.

⚠What to avoid

  • Locking or hyperextending your knees

  • Letting your weight shift entirely to your heels

  • Collapsing your chest or rounding your upper back

  • Holding your breath — keep it slow and even

Pro tips

💡

Press down through your feet as if rooting into the earth, and feel the lift travel up through your spine.

💡

Close your eyes briefly to tune into subtle weight imbalances between your left and right sides.

💡

Think 'tall' — imagine a thread gently pulling the crown of your head toward the ceiling.
