Savasana

Corpse Pose

SupineBeginner
⏱Hold 5–15 minutes
Full BodyNervous System

Overview

Savasana is practiced at the end of most yoga sessions. Though it looks like simply lying down, it is one of the most challenging poses — the goal is complete conscious relaxation of body and mind, integrating the benefits of the entire practice.

  • ✓Deeply relaxes the entire body and nervous system
  • ✓Reduces stress, anxiety, and blood pressure
  • ✓Integrates the benefits of the yoga session
  • ✓May improve sleep quality
  • ✓Cultivates a meditative, present-moment awareness

How to do it

  1. 1

    Lie flat on your back on the mat. Let your feet fall open naturally.

  2. 2

    Bring your arms slightly away from your body, palms facing up.

  3. 3

    Close your eyes and let your breath return to normal.

  4. 4

    Systematically relax each body part: feet, calves, thighs, hips, belly, chest, hands, arms, shoulders, neck, face.

  5. 5

    Let your jaw drop slightly, your tongue rest on the floor of your mouth, and your eyes remain still.

  6. 6

    Remain here for 5–15 minutes, observing sensations without reacting.

  7. 7

    To come out, wiggle your fingers and toes, then roll to your right side before slowly sitting up.

⚠What to avoid

  • Skipping Savasana — it is as important as any active pose

  • Falling into a deep sleep unless you intend a yoga nidra practice

  • Fidgeting — resist the urge to scratch or adjust; observe the sensation instead

  • A cold environment — cover yourself with a blanket to stay warm

Pro tips

💡

Use an eye pillow or folded cloth over your eyes to block light and deepen relaxation.

💡

Place a rolled blanket under your knees to release tension in the lower back.

💡

Set a gentle timer so you don't worry about the time, and can surrender fully.
