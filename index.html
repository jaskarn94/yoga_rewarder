Eka Pada Rajakapotasana

Pigeon Pose

Hip OpenerIntermediate
⏱Hold 1–3 minutes each side
HipsGlutesPiriformisHip Flexors

Overview

Pigeon Pose is one of the deepest hip-opening postures in yoga. It targets the outer hip and piriformis on the bent-leg side while stretching the hip flexor of the extended leg. Many students find it emotionally releasing due to the intensity of the hip area.

  • ✓Deeply opens the outer hip and glutes
  • ✓Stretches the hip flexors and psoas
  • ✓Relieves sciatic nerve pain in some cases
  • ✓Increases external rotation of the hip
  • ✓Can release deeply held emotional tension stored in the hips

How to do it

  1. 1

    From Downward Dog, bring your right knee forward toward your right wrist.

  2. 2

    Slide your right foot toward your left wrist — the more parallel your shin is to the front of the mat, the more intense the stretch.

  3. 3

    Extend your left leg straight back, top of foot on the mat, toes pointing back.

  4. 4

    Square your hips toward the front of the mat as much as possible.

  5. 5

    Inhale and lengthen your spine. Exhale and fold forward over your front leg.

  6. 6

    Rest your forehead on your hands or on the mat. Breathe deeply for 1–3 minutes.

  7. 7

    Press back into Downward Dog and repeat on the left side.

⚠What to avoid

  • Letting your hips tilt heavily to one side — use a folded blanket under the hip

  • Collapsing into the pose without engaging — keep some tone in your abs

  • Forcing your front shin parallel if your hips are tight — start with the foot closer to your hip

  • Ignoring knee pain — if you feel it in the knee (not hip), come out and try Figure-4 instead

Pro tips

💡

Place a folded blanket or block under your right hip to keep your pelvis level.

💡

Stay for longer holds (3–5 minutes) to allow the connective tissue around the hip to release.

💡

Figure-4 / Supine Pigeon is an excellent preparatory pose if this feels too intense.
