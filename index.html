Trikonasana

Triangle Pose

StandingBeginner
⏱Hold 30–60 seconds each side
HamstringsHipsSpineCore

Overview

Triangle Pose is a foundational standing pose that stretches the hamstrings, groin, and hips while opening the chest and strengthening the legs. Its triangular geometry promotes length along both sides of the body simultaneously.

  • ✓Stretches hamstrings, calves, and hips
  • ✓Strengthens thighs, knees, and ankles
  • ✓Opens the chest and shoulders
  • ✓Stimulates abdominal organs and digestion
  • ✓Reduces stress and anxiety

How to do it

  1. 1

    Stand with feet wide apart (about 3.5 feet). Turn your right foot out 90° and left foot in 15°.

  2. 2

    Extend your arms to the sides at shoulder height.

  3. 3

    On an exhale, reach your right hand forward as you hinge at the hip — not the waist.

  4. 4

    Lower your right hand to your shin, ankle, or a block. Extend your left arm straight up.

  5. 5

    Open your chest toward the ceiling. Gaze up at your top thumb or straight ahead.

  6. 6

    Keep both legs straight and press firmly through both feet.

  7. 7

    Hold, then inhale to come up. Repeat on the other side.

⚠What to avoid

  • Rounding your spine to reach lower — use a block if needed

  • Collapsing your chest toward the floor instead of opening it

  • Locking your front knee — keep a micro-bend

  • Letting your top hip roll forward — stack hips one above the other

Pro tips

💡

Think of this as a side-body opener, not a hamstring stretch — the spine stays long.

💡

Place a block on the outside of your front foot to bring the floor up to you.

💡

Press the outer edge of your back foot firmly into the mat for stability.
