Urdhva Dhanurasana

Full Wheel Pose

BackbendAdvanced
⏱Hold 15–30 seconds, 1–3 rounds
SpineShouldersChestArmsGlutes

Overview

Full Wheel is one of the most expansive backbends in yoga. It opens the entire front body and demands strength from the arms, legs, and core simultaneously. It is a pose that cultivates both physical power and emotional openness.

  • ✓Deeply opens the chest, spine, and hip flexors
  • ✓Strengthens the arms, wrists, legs, and glutes
  • ✓Stimulates the nervous system and increases energy
  • ✓Stretches the lungs and improves breathing capacity
  • ✓Builds courage and cultivates a sense of exhilaration

How to do it

  1. 1

    Lie on your back. Bend your knees and place feet flat on the floor, hip-width apart and close to your hips.

  2. 2

    Bend your elbows and place your palms on the floor beside your head, fingers pointing toward your shoulders.

  3. 3

    On an inhale, press into your hands and feet and lift your hips and chest toward the ceiling.

  4. 4

    Straighten your arms fully and let your head hang between your arms.

  5. 5

    Press your feet firmly into the mat and energize your legs.

  6. 6

    Hold for 15–30 seconds, breathing fully. Lower with control.

  7. 7

    Rest in Knees-to-Chest Pose between rounds.

⚠What to avoid

  • Practicing without sufficient shoulder mobility — bridge pose is a safer alternative

  • Letting your elbows flare wide — keep them tracking parallel

  • Compressing the lower back — press into the floor with your feet and squeeze your glutes

  • Practicing with wrist injuries or acute back problems

Pro tips

💡

Warm up with Bridge Pose, Camel, and Cobra before attempting Full Wheel.

💡

If you cannot fully straighten your arms, place your hands on blocks to reduce the angle.

💡

After coming down, hug your knees to your chest and do a gentle supine twist to counterpose.
