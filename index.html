Navasana

Boat Pose

BalancingIntermediate
⏱Hold 20–60 seconds, 3–5 rounds
CoreHip FlexorsSpineLegs

Overview

Boat Pose is a challenging core and balance posture that strengthens the deep abdominal muscles, hip flexors, and spinal erectors. Balancing on your sit bones while extending legs and arms demands full-body engagement.

  • ✓Deeply strengthens the core and hip flexors
  • ✓Improves balance and body coordination
  • ✓Stimulates the kidneys, thyroid, and intestines
  • ✓Builds confidence and willpower
  • ✓Tones the abdominal organs

How to do it

  1. 1

    Sit on your mat with knees bent, feet flat on the floor.

  2. 2

    Lean back slightly until you feel your core engage. Place your hands behind your thighs.

  3. 3

    Lift your feet off the floor so your shins are parallel to the ground.

  4. 4

    If stable, extend your arms forward parallel to the floor, palms facing up.

  5. 5

    For the full pose, slowly straighten your legs to a 45-degree angle.

  6. 6

    Keep your spine long — avoid rounding your lower back.

  7. 7

    Hold for 20–60 seconds, then lower on an exhale. Rest and repeat.

⚠What to avoid

  • Rounding your lower back — if it rounds, bend your knees or hold the backs of your thighs

  • Holding your breath — steady breathing is essential

  • Collapsing your chest — keep it lifted and broad

  • Straining your neck — let it stay in line with your spine

Pro tips

💡

Bend your knees throughout if the full expression is too intense — it's still deeply effective.

💡

Try variations: 'low boat' (lowering to hover) and 'high boat' to build strength dynamically.

💡

Press the soles of your feet together if you prefer a variation that targets inner thighs.
